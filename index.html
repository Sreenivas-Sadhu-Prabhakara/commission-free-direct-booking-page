How it works

The page is priced one night at a time. Each night from the arrival date to the night before departure is looked up against your seasons, and the first season that covers it sets that night’s rate as the room’s rate plus or minus your percentage. A stay that runs into a peak period is therefore split automatically: two nights at the standard rate and two at the peak rate, not four at whichever rate happened to apply on the day of arrival.

On top of the nights come the people the rate does not cover. Adults beyond the number the rate covers are charged your extra-adult rate for every night. Children are charged your child rate for every night, except those below the free age, who are charged nothing — but every child still counts towards how many the room sleeps, because a bed is a bed. If the party is larger than the room, the page says so and refuses to build an enquiry link rather than quote an impossible booking.

Tax is applied last, at the rate you entered, and you say whether your rates already include it. If they do, the tax is worked backwards out of the total rather than added to it, so the figure a guest sees is the figure you quoted.

In short: nights = checkout − checkin, room = Σ rate × (1 + season%) for each night, extras = extra adults × rate × nights + paying children × rate × nights, total = (room + extras) × (1 + tax%) when tax is added, or total = room + extras with tax = total × rate ÷ (100 + rate) when it is already inside. The deposit is a percentage of that total.

Why the link matters more than the page

A booking page without a server cannot hold a reservation, and this tool does not pretend otherwise. What it can do is make the first message arrive complete. The WhatsApp link is built by percent-encoding the whole message, which is what keeps a space, an ampersand, a plus sign, a newline, an emoji or a name in Devanagari, Kannada or Tamil intact on the way. A plus sign left unencoded in a web address is read back as a space, so Jo+Ann becomes Jo Ann and 2+2 guests becomes 2 2 guests — the kind of fault nobody reports, because the guest simply gives up. The link is shown here in full, before you publish it, and an email version is built the same way with the line endings mail clients expect.

Assumptions & rates
  • No tax rate is built in. Accommodation tax is a state matter in India and a city or municipal matter in the Philippines, and both change. The rate and its name are yours to enter; zero means none is shown.
  • Nights are counted as dates, not as hours: arrival to departure, with the departure night not charged. Dates are handled as plain calendar dates throughout, so no time zone or daylight change can move a night.
  • Each night takes the rate of the first season listed that covers it. Overlaps are reported, and the season listed first wins. A season whose dates are unusable, or that ends before it begins, is ignored.
  • A season rate change below −100% is held at −100%, so a night can be free but never negative.
  • Extra-adult and child rates are flat: they are not changed by the season.
  • Children below the free age are not charged but are still counted against how many the room sleeps. A child exactly at the free age pays.
  • If the rate-covers field is blank it is read as one adult; if sleeps is blank the occupancy limit is not checked, and both are reported to you.
  • The deposit is a percentage of the final total including tax, and the balance is the rest. The page states the deposit; it cannot take it.
  • A booking is refused, with the reason shown and no link built, when the departure is before the arrival, when both dates are the same day, when no adult is on the booking, when a date is not a real date, or when the party is larger than the room.
  • WhatsApp numbers are reduced to digits with the country code in front, because that is all wa.me accepts. A leading + or 00 is read as a full international number; a leading 0 is dropped as a trunk prefix; a ten-digit Indian or Philippine number that happens to begin with its own country code is not mistaken for one that already carries it.
  • Money is rounded to two decimal places at each line and shown whole when it is whole.
An estimate and a brochure, not a reservation system. The page you download cannot take a payment, cannot hold a room and cannot check availability: it states your rates and hands a complete enquiry to you, and the booking exists when you reply. Check the total against your own tariff card before you publish, and confirm the tax position with your own accountant or your local authority. Nothing you type here leaves your device, and the page you get collects nothing from your guests.
